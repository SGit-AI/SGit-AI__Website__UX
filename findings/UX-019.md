# Search only matches titles, teasers and tags

Open · low severity · gap · newsroom.sgit.ai

## What we saw

The search box does not look inside articles, although a markdown twin of every article exists.

## Why it matters

About 300,000 words are unsearchable; a reader who remembers a phrase cannot find the piece.

## The fix

Search over bodies and abstracts from the markdown twins, with snippets.
