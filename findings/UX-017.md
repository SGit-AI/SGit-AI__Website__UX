# There is no "start here" path

Open · medium severity · gap · newsroom.sgit.ai

## What we saw

The Historian's weekly note and the collections are the best orientation on the site, and they sit below the fold.

## Why it matters

A newcomer facing 73 long articles has no way to know which five to read first.

## The fix

Each desk opens with "Start here: five articles in order", written by the Historian.
