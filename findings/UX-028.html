Findings / UX-028

The £5.00 balance chip has no label

OpenHighFailurenewsroom.sgit.ai

What we saw

An unlabelled "£5.00" sits in the header of every page. Still unlabelled in v0.7.1.

Why it matters

An amount of money with no explanation reads as a bill or a trap; the synthetic-user study flagged it.

The fix

Label it "£5 free reading credit".
