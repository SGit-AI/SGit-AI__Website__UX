Findings / UX-028

The £5.00 balance chip has no label

Partly fixedHighFailurenewsroom.sgit.ai

What we saw

An unlabelled "£5.00" sits in the header of every page. Still unlabelled in v0.7.1.

Why it matters

An amount of money with no explanation reads as a bill or a trap; the synthetic-user study flagged it.

The fix

Label it "£5 free reading credit".

The record

StatusPartly fixed. Partly fixed in v0.12.120: a meter bar on the front page reads "0/6 read · £1.67 spent · £3.33 left", but the header chip on every page is still a bare "£3.33". Seen at iPad width, 18 Oct.
