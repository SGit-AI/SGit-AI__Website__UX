# The version number is shown three times on the first screen

Open · low severity · failure · newsroom.sgit.ai

## What we saw

v0.7.1 appears three times on the desktop first screen: in the agent strip, the metadata lines and the footer badge.

## Why it matters

Engineering detail repeated where a reader looks first; it reads as a demo, not a publication.

## The fix

One version, in the workshop and the page foot, linked to what changed. None on the reader's first screen.
