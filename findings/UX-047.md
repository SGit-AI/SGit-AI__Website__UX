# localStorage throws inside the vault app frame, so reader data is lost on reload

Open · medium severity · failure · dev.vault.sgraph.ai

## What we saw

The app frame refuses localStorage with SecurityError. Lab kit v0.1.2 kept highlights, notes and ratings only until reload.

## Why it matters

A reader's notes disappear on reload, the worst failure a note-taking feature can have.

## The fix

Store through the host's async sg.state (survives reload). Kit v0.1.3 does; every lab vault was republished on it.
