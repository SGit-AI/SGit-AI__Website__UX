# Persona links were designed but not live

Fixed · high severity · gap · newsroom.sgit.ai

## What we saw

Persona links (/for/ciso and others) were described in articles but no URL existed to send a contact.

## Why it matters

Every contact got the same front page, made for nobody in particular.

## The fix

Ship persona links for the main audiences.
