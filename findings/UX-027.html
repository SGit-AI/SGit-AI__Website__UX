Findings / UX-027

Articles join the reader's list without being asked

OpenMediumFailurenewsroom.sgit.ai

What we saw

Opening an article adds it to the reader's reading list automatically.

Why it matters

The list stops meaning "what I chose to keep" and becomes a history the reader did not ask for.

The fix

Make "Keep" a tap.
