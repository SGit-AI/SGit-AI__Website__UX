# The masthead line describes a method, not a subject

Open · medium severity · gap · newsroom.sgit.ai

## What we saw

The tagline is "one page, one argument, with the figures, the data and the links to check it". Nothing says agents, AI governance, security or news economics.

## Why it matters

The one line every visitor reads tells them how the site works instead of what it is about.

## The fix

A masthead line that names the subject and the author, e.g. "Field notes on AI agents, security and the next generation of publishing, by Dinis Cruz".
