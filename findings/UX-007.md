# The editor's note is written for someone who read the last two editions

Open · low severity · failure · newsroom.sgit.ai

## What we saw

The note opens "Third edition... the last two days", assuming the reader has followed along.

## Why it matters

The one personal voice on the front page talks past the newcomer the page most needs to win.

## The fix

Write the note for a newcomer: what this edition is about and where to start.
