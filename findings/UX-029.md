# Opening an article charges a penny with no notice

Open · high severity · failure · newsroom.sgit.ai

## What we saw

The chip changes from £5.00 to £4.99 on opening any article, silently. In the synthetic-user study this exact moment made a retired reader ask whether she owed money.

## Why it matters

The first thing a new reader learns about the reading meter is that their balance dropped without being told.

## The fix

Announce the first charge once, in words, at the moment it happens.
