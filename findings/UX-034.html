Findings / UX-034

No contact for a pilot on the Replicate page

OpenHighGapnewsroom.sgit.ai

What we saw

The page that invites publishers to replicate the newsroom has no name, email or form.

Why it matters

The one reader ready to act has nowhere to go. A blocking finding of the synthetic-user study.

The fix

A name and email for pilots on Replicate and on the account pages; move the publishers page to the newsroom with the same contact.
