Findings / UX-006

A "For agents /llms.txt" strip sits above the masthead

OpenLowFailurenewsroom.sgit.ai

What we saw

The first element on every page is a strip addressed to agents, above the publication's name.

Why it matters

Useful to agents, noise to a person, and it takes the most valuable line on the screen.

The fix

Move it to the page head (link rel) and the footer, where agents look anyway. Satisfies both the Architect and the Designer on the panel.
