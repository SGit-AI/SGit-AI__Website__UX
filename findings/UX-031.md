# "You pay the price" reads as a warning

Open · low severity · failure · newsroom.sgit.ai

## What we saw

The caption under the usefulness scale reads "You pay the price".

## Why it matters

The feature (your rating sets what the page costs you) is a benefit; the wording makes it a threat.

## The fix

"Your rating sets what this page costs you".
