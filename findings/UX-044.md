# The founder page says "the picks above" and no picks render on a phone

Open · high severity · failure · newsroom.sgit.ai

## What we saw

On a phone with no prior visit, the founder persona page refers to picks that are not shown.

## Why it matters

The page a founder is sent to points at content that is not there, on the first visit, which is the only one that matters.

## The fix

Render the picks on the landing view with no prior state; test it in a fresh browser.
