# Opening a persona link adds it to the browser without asking

Open · medium severity · failure · newsroom.sgit.ai

## What we saw

Opening a persona page silently adds the persona to the reader's browser.

## Why it matters

The reader did not choose to become "a founder" on this site; it was done to them.

## The fix

Show the persona and offer "Read as" and "Keep" as separate choices.
