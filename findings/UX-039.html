Findings / UX-039

The feed is titled for the old site

OpenLowFailurenewsroom.sgit.ai

What we saw

articles/feed.xml is titled "sgit.ai articles".

Why it matters

Subscribers see the wrong publication name in their reader.

The fix

Retitle it "SGit Newsroom", from the same source as the masthead.
