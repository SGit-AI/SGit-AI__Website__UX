Findings / UX-035

The top-up page said "simulated" while asking for money

FixedHighFailurenewsroom.sgit.ai

What we saw

The synthetic-user run of 10 October found the top-up page asking for a payment while describing itself as simulated.

Why it matters

A payment page that says it is not real is a payment page nobody can trust.

The fix

Describe the one real Stripe payment. Done in v0.6.4; real Stripe top-up live since 11 October.
