# One topic holds more than half the articles

Open · medium severity · failure · newsroom.sgit.ai

## What we saw

"Agents & policy" holds 35 of 67 articles (38 of 73 in the lab's snapshot). The other five topics hold 13 to 26 and overlap: an article sits in about two.

## Why it matters

A CISO who picks the obvious topic still faces 35 long reads. The classification does not help anyone choose.

## The fix

Desks by audience, not internal topic: security, building, vaults, news, strategy (18 / 17 / 14 / 15 / 9 in the lab).
