# A highlight across paragraphs is cut at the first paragraph

Open · low severity · failure · Newsroom Design Lab

## What we saw

Selecting text that spans two paragraphs and tapping Highlight keeps only the part in the first.

## Why it matters

The reader loses part of what they chose to keep, without being told.

## The fix

Store highlights as ranges across blocks, or refuse the selection with a message.
