Findings / UX-010

Missing pages show the default GitHub Pages 404

OpenMediumFailurenewsroom.sgit.ai

What we saw

Any missing URL shows GitHub's generic error page, with no link back to the newsroom.

Why it matters

A mistyped or old link ends the visit on a page that does not even say which site it is.

The fix

A branded 404.html with search and the three entry doors.
