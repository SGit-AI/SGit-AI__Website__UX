Findings / UX-056

Scrolling back up a little puts focus in the address bar and opens the keyboard

OpenHighFailureFirefox on iPad

What we saw

Reading a long page, a small scroll back up brings the hidden toolbars back, the address field gets focus with its URL selected, and the on-screen keyboard opens over the bottom half of the page (second screenshot: the URL selected, Cancel showing, the keyboard covering the release table). Nothing on the page was tapped on purpose. A likely mechanism, not yet confirmed: the toolbar slides in under the finger mid-gesture, so the touch that was a scroll lands on the address field as a tap.

Why it matters

The reader was not asking to go anywhere, and loses half the screen to a keyboard. Worse, it looks like the page has a bug: the first reading was that the site had somehow started an edit. The browser's fault is charged to the site, and the reader's concentration is broken either way.

The fix

For the browser: a scroll gesture never focuses the address field; only a deliberate tap on a toolbar that is already at rest does. For the sites: nothing a page can prevent, but worth knowing that readers will report it as our bug, so it is recorded here with its real owner.
