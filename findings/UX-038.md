# Three different article counts

Open · medium severity · failure · newsroom.sgit.ai

## What we saw

sgit.ai said 72, the newsroom home said 67, llms.txt said 66, on the same day.

## Why it matters

When a site cannot count itself, the reader doubts every other number on it.

## The fix

Generate every count from one manifest at build, and fail the release if any page disagrees.
