Findings / UX-045

Persona pages are a list, with no story, opinion or next step

OpenMediumGapnewsroom.sgit.ai

What we saw

Each persona page is seven starting articles plus an explanation of how the link works.

Why it matters

A persona link is a promise that the site was made for this reader; a list does not keep it.

The fix

One front-page template filled from a persona file: masthead, story, Dinis's note, start here, sections, board, "what is missing?", other readers.
