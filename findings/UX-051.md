# Re-rendering the whole view on every save lost the reader's place

Fixed · medium severity · failure · Newsroom Design Lab

## What we saw

Every store write (a highlight, a note, a status) re-rendered the whole reading view and jumped the scroll position.

## Why it matters

A reader who highlights a sentence is thrown somewhere else in an 8,000-word article.

## The fix

Repaint only the parts that changed. Done in the article vault before publishing.
