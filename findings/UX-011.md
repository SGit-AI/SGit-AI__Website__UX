# Links without extensions in one article

Open · low severity · failure · newsroom.sgit.ai

## What we saw

future-of-news links to /thesis, /portugal and /economics/rails, unlike every other link on the site.

## Why it matters

They depend on the host resolving extensionless paths; on a strict host or a mirror they break.

## The fix

Use the full .html or trailing-slash form everywhere; check it at build.
