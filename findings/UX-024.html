Findings / UX-024

All three rating scales start on 3

OpenHighFailurenewsroom.sgit.ai

What we saw

The usefulness, more-like-this and level-of-detail scales at the foot of every article are preselected on the middle value.

Why it matters

Neither the reader nor the author can tell "not rated" from "useful". Every rating collected so far is ambiguous.

The fix

Start unselected; record only real taps.
