Findings / UX-032

Nothing shows real money moving

OpenHighGapnewsroom.sgit.ai

What we saw

No counts of readers, payments or revenue anywhere. A blocking finding of the synthetic-user study, still open.

Why it matters

Readers and publishers asking "is this a real business?" find no answer.

The fix

Public reader numbers (ratings, readers, payments), generated and dated.
