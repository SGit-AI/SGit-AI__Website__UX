# Two domains claim to be canonical for the same article

Open · high severity · failure · newsroom.sgit.ai

## What we saw

sgit.ai/articles/hope-or-enforcement.html declares canonical sgit.ai; the newsroom copy declares canonical newsroom.sgit.ai.

## Why it matters

Search engines split the article's standing between two copies, and readers can land on either.

## The fix

One canonical, the newsroom; a 301 or a canonical pointing there from sgit.ai.
