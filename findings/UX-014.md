# Tags are not links, and there are no tag pages

Open · medium severity · failure · newsroom.sgit.ai

## What we saw

The lead article carries 12 tag pills; none links anywhere. Tags mix subjects, formats and product names ("article", "riskmandate"). 339 raw tags, 228 used once.

## Why it matters

The most precise way into the archive looks clickable and goes nowhere.

## The fix

A controlled vocabulary of about 40 tags owned by the Librarian, a page per tag, tags as links capped at five per article (339 → 42 in the lab; median 11 → 4).
