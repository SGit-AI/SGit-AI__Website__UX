# Two navigation bars, and a 60-link menu that mixes reading with building

Open · medium severity · failure · newsroom.sgit.ai

## What we saw

A second bar of nine links repeats the menu. The menu holds about 60 links in nine groups (57 links in 8 groups when the lab mapped it), mixing reader pages (Newsletter, Collections) with builder pages (Replicate, Admin, Release history, The run log).

## Why it matters

The reader has to sort the publication from the workshop themselves, twice.

## The fix

One navigation. The publication on top; the workshop behind one item with its own front page.
