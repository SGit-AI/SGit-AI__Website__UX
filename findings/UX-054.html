Findings / UX-054

Stacked tables on a phone lose their column labels

OpenLowGapNewsroom Design Lab

What we saw

At 390 px the prototype stacks table rows into blocks without repeating the column names.

Why it matters

Values without labels on a phone: the reader has to remember what each line means.

The fix

Repeat the column label in each stacked cell (data-label + ::before).
