# File-path pills scrolled the whole page sideways at 390 px

Fixed · medium severity · failure · Newsroom Design Lab

## What we saw

Long, unbreakable file-path pills on the data pages pushed the page wider than a phone.

## Why it matters

Sideways scroll on a phone makes every line harder to read and hides the edge of the content.

## The fix

Let pills wrap; the gate now fails on any sideways scroll at 390 px. Fixed in kit v0.1.2.
