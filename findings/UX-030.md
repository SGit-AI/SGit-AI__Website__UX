# "Credit can go below zero" reads as a debt

Open · medium severity · failure · newsroom.sgit.ai

## What we saw

The account page says credit can go below zero.

## Why it matters

Readers hear "you can owe us money", on a site that has not asked them for any.

## The fix

Say what actually happens when the free credit is used up, in reader terms.
