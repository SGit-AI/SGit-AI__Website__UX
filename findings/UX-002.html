Findings / UX-002

The home page is 59 phone screens long, with 845 links

OpenHighFailurenewsroom.sgit.ai

What we saw

The phone home page measures 49,715 px: Latest, Highlights, desk notes, collections, the full article list, then a map of every article's links.

Why it matters

The front page tries to show everything, so it chooses nothing for the reader. Nobody scrolls 59 screens; the curation is invisible.

The fix

Cut the home page to the lead, three per desk, collections and the newsletter. Lists and the link map move to their own pages.
