# Articles were published on two domains, and diverged

Fixed · high severity · failure · newsroom.sgit.ai

## What we saw

sgit.ai/articles/ served five 11 October articles (e.g. no-server-by-design, the-agent-is-the-reader) that returned 404 on newsroom.sgit.ai.

## Why it matters

The same publication was more complete on the wrong site.

## The fix

Publish once to the newsroom; make sgit.ai link or redirect there.
