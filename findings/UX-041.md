# 51 vault images missing across 10 articles

Fixed · high severity · failure · newsroom.sgit.ai

## What we saw

The third synthetic-user run found 51 vault images returning 404 in 10 articles.

## Why it matters

Evidence images are the point of an evidence-first article; broken ones undo the claim.

## The fix

Fixed: 0 broken images on 10 rendered articles in v0.6.4.
