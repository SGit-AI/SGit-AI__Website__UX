# Ratings never reach the author

Open · high severity · gap · newsroom.sgit.ai

## What we saw

Ratings stay in the reader's browser unless they choose to send their whole reading record.

## Why it matters

The first contacts sent to the site teach the author nothing about what landed.

## The fix

One-tap send by default, with plain consent ("Send this rating to Dinis, anonymous"), through the encrypted append lane, with a local receipt.
