How a finding gets here

Someone uses the site as a stranger would: a phone, a link, three minutes. What gets in their way is written down with the evidence, the date and the version, then named against a principle. The method has the details. The first source was the newsroom UX review of 11 October 2026; new findings come from the same kind of look at any site in the network.

This site follows its own principles, or it is a finding too: the first screen says what it is, every view is a link, every count is generated from one file, and the content is there without JavaScript.
