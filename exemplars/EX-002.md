# Dictation that shows your words as you speak them

## What it does

While you dictate, the Claude app writes the transcript into the message box as you speak, then refines it in a second pass when you stop. The first pass is rough, but it is immediate.

## Why it works

Seeing your words appear is proof that they are being captured. It is the reason the reporter uses this input over others: as long as the text keeps appearing, they know the recording is safe, and they can keep their attention on what they are saying.

## What it still does not do

When the live pass stalls (UX-063), the proof disappears with no explanation, and the second pass rewrites text without showing what changed (UX-062). The feedback is the right idea; it needs to cover the failure cases too.
