Reviews

A review is a dated, whole look at one site. The findings it produces go into the register, where their status is kept current; the review itself is published as it was written and not edited afterwards, so it stays a record of what was true on its date.

DateSiteVersionsReview
11 Oct 2026newsroom.sgit.aiv0.6.4, re-checked on v0.7.1Newsroom UX review: first visit, findability, the article and its feedback, synthetic users, benchmarks, a six-role panel, the publishing pipeline, the direction, personas, reader primitives and a roadmap

What a review here contains

  • Measured, not recalled. A crawl, a real browser at phone and desktop sizes, and the numbers that came out of them.
  • A panel of roles, each asking its own question of the same evidence: architect, librarian, historian, designer, editor, journalist.
  • Benchmarks from established sites that have solved the same problem, with sources.
  • A roadmap ordered by one rule: fix what breaks trust first, then build the doors, then grow.

The method describes how to run one.
